{# SHARED CHROME -- the first-paint theme bootstrap, inlined into by both consoles (`helios_ui_chrome::theme_boot()`). Deliberately inline, and deliberately tiny: it must run before the first paint, without waiting on a network round trip, or a dark preference flashes light. It only *reads* the preference (shared cookie, then the pre-cookie localStorage cache) and stamps `data-theme-pref` (system | light | dark) and the effective `data-theme` (light | dark) on . Everything else -- cycling, persistence, migration, cross-tab and cross-console sync, animation -- lives in `crates/ui/assets/theme.js`. #} {#- -#}
